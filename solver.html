📘 KTaNE Defusal Operations Manual

Complete guide for all 14 modules. Includes fast keyboard shortcuts, screenshot-to-solve tips, occurrence rules, and edge-case reminders.

⚡ Universal Speed-Defusal Tips

📋 Instant Screenshot Pasting (Ctrl+V):
Hit Win+Shift+S or PrtScn in-game, click on any module tab, and press Ctrl+V. The solver processes OCR and image recognition automatically!
⌨️ Fast Wire Sequences Keyboard Input:
On the Wire Sequences tab, type shorthand like 1RA 2RB 3BC or 1BLACKC and press Enter. Hit Shift+Enter to jump straight to the next stage!
📝 Dynamic Rules Seed Support:
Playing a custom rule seed? Modules featuring game manual variations have editable rule textareas that parse changes on the fly.

🧵 Wire Sequences

Wires

Overview: 5 consecutive panels with up to 3 wires each. Occurrence counts (1st Red, 2nd Red, etc.) accumulate across all 5 panels.

Fast Input: Type in the quick input box: 1RA 2RB 3BC (Wire 1 Red->A, Wire 2 Red->B, Wire 3 Blue->C) or single wires like 1BLACKC and hit Enter.

Controls: Click Next Stage ➔ (or press Shift+Enter) to advance. Clear Stage clears current panel wires.

Screenshot: Paste panel screenshot (Ctrl+V) for automatic wire detection.

🔴🟡 Maze Solver

Puzzles

Overview: Finds the shortest path from start dot (Red) to target dot (Yellow) through circular/grid mazes.

Slot 1 (Dots): Click Slot 1, paste or upload screenshot with the dots visible.

Slot 2 (Maze): Click Slot 2, paste or upload screenshot of the maze walls.

Result: Solves automatically in milliseconds, rendering the bright green path and printing exact directional steps (e.g. UP 2, RIGHT 3).

⭕ Venn / Complicated Wires

Wires

Overview: Decides whether to cut each wire based on colors (Red/Blue/White), Star symbol (★), and LED light.

How to Solve: Paste the Venn diagram screenshot (Ctrl+V) or click regions on the canvas to toggle shaded/unshaded.

Cut Letters: C = Cut, D = Do Not Cut, S = Cut if serial even, P = Cut if parallel port, B = Cut if 2+ batteries.

⚡ Logic Solver

Puzzles

Overview: Evaluates boolean propositional logic equations for 4 sequential stages (α, β, γ, δ).

Variables: Paste variable assignments (e.g. P = T, Q = T, R = F) in the sidebar or add individual custom variables.

Stages: Enter expression (or paste screenshot directly into the input). Live preview validates syntax and computes the output immediately.

📻 Morse Code

Signals

Overview: An orange light blinks a repeating word in Morse code. Find the word and tune to its frequency.

Input: Watch the light and tap dots (.) and dashes (-) or enter letters. Solver narrows down the 16 possible words.

Transmission: Tune the radio dial to the matching frequency (between 3.505 MHz and 3.600 MHz) and press TX.

🎚️ Switches Module

Wires

Overview: 5 toggle switches with 5 LEDs above them. Flip switches to reach the target LED state without short-circuiting.

Input: Toggle switch handles to match current bomb state, and click target LEDs to match goal pattern.

Solution: Solver outputs the safe step-by-step flip order with Prev/Next step-by-step navigation.

🔍 Search / Word Finder

Words

Overview: Ultra-fast dictionary and pattern solver for 5-letter password puzzles and letter grids.

Pattern Search: Use dots for unknown letters (e.g. ..A.T) to find matches like PLANT, SMART.

Speed Tip: Entering just 2 or 3 known letters usually isolates the solution word immediately.

🎛️ Knobs Solver

Signals

Overview: 12 LEDs arranged in two 3x2 grids (left and right). The lit LED pattern dictates the rotary knob direction.

Manual Picture: The table changes every game — paste the manual picture (UP / DOWN / LEFT / RIGHT panels) once with Ctrl+V; it is read into the table and remembered for the game.

How to Solve: Click the LEDs on the screen to match the lit lights on the bomb.

Result: Highlights the exact direction: UP, DOWN, LEFT, or RIGHT.

🧠 Memory Module

Words

Overview: 5 stages. You must remember earlier button positions and button labels across each stage.

Rule Note: Position = 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th button from left. Label = number printed on the button face.

Automation: Enter the display number and 4 button labels. The solver highlights which button to press and auto-stores your history!

🔑 Password Solver

Words

Overview: 5 letter dials. Find the single word from the official 35-word dictionary that can be formed.

Fast Defusal: Check column 1 and column 2 letters first. Enter them in the solver to eliminate nearly all invalid candidates.

Result: Instantly displays the solution word. Set each dial to that word and click Submit.

🎹 Piano Keys

Signals

Overview: A musical staff shows 3 notes with either a Treble Clef (🎼) or Bass Clef (𝄢).

Input: Paste screenshot (Ctrl+V) or pick clef and note positions manually.

Playback: Identifies the chord sequence and highlights the exact piano keys to play in sequence.

🔘 The Button (Dynamic Timing)

Puzzles

Overview: Solves countdown release timings (5s, 7s, 10s, 11s, 12s, etc.) across button color, label, needy count, non-needy count, disarmed modules, and serial number rules.

Dual-Option Highlighting: If you leave parameters like non-needy count or disarmed modules empty/unknown, the solver automatically evaluates both variations (e.g. 5s if Odd vs 7s if Even) and highlights both solutions side-by-side!

Live Serial Stats: Automatically calculates vowels count, rightmost digit parity (even/odd), and digits vs letters balance in real time.

❓ Who's on First

Words

Overview: Classic two-step word puzzle. Display word references a button position; button word references a priority list.

Step 1: Display word tells you which button position to read (e.g. BLANK = Top-Left, FIRST = Bottom-Right).

Step 2: Read that button's word and press the first word in its priority list that appears on any of the 6 buttons.

Auto-OCR: Paste module screenshot (Ctrl+V) for instant OCR reading and green button highlight!

🔌 Circuit Solver

Wires

Overview: A network of coloured resistors between two orange terminals — the answer is the total resistance (Ω) between them.

How to Solve: Paste the Circuit screenshot: every resistor, wire and terminal is read from the picture and the circuit is solved exactly (series, parallel, shorted corners, bridges). The answer is copied for you.

Colour Legend: The colour → Ω table changes every game. Paste its picture (Ctrl+V) on the Circuit tab once per game — same drop zone as the circuit. Fix a misread value in the legend boxes; the reader learns that digit.

Speed Tip: Glance at the boxed resistors under "What was read" — if a colour is wrong, fix it in the table and the answer updates.

🗝️ Limbo

Puzzles

Overview: Six keys in a ring; one glows, then pairs swap. The last frame reveals every key's colour.

How to Solve: Drag in the whole GIF (or its Discord link). The glowing key is followed through every swap and its final colour is the answer.

Speed Tip: If no glow is found, tap the start key on the board to re-run from it. A copied GIF often pastes as a still frame — drag it instead.

🟩 Pipes

Puzzles

Overview: Each colour button turns every pipe of that colour 90°. Solved when all pipes form one network with no loops and no open ends.

How to Solve: Paste the helper's coloured picture (Ctrl+V). You get how many times to press each colour, for both ↻ clockwise and ↺ anticlockwise bombs.

Speed Tip: The defuser's picture never updates — press the counts and don't re-check. Tap a tile to fix a misread colour.

✨ Astrology

Puzzles

Overview: Count the stars; every constellation with that many stars is looked up on the sky map. Press the one button in the same quadrant as one of them.

Each Game: Paste the helper's wide sky map and the star list text (Ctrl+V anywhere on the tab). Both change every game — press New Game to forget them.

Each Bomb: Paste the defuser's screenshot. Stars are counted and the 4 symbols named; fix the count or a symbol by hand if one is marked unsure.

🪢 Hangman

Words

Overview: Every letter guess draws a body part, right or wrong — so the solver picks the letter that narrows the manual's word list down fastest, not the most common one.

How to Solve: Guess the shown letter, then paste the defuser's screenshot (Ctrl+V) — new letters show where it is; nothing new = a miss. Once one word is left, guess the whole word.

Speed Tip: Paste this bomb's manual text anywhere on the tab to load its word list. No screenshot? Tap the outcome button that matches what the blanks showed.

🔴 Simon Says

Signals

Overview: Each stage adds one flash. The manual is one fixed table; its row depends on the serial having a vowel and on an odd or even number of disarmed modules.

How to Solve: Type the bomb serial once, then paste each stage's GIF (Ctrl+V) — its last flash is the new colour. Press the ODD or EVEN sequence shown.

Speed Tip: Drop several stage files at once — they're read in file-name order. Tap a colour on a stage to fix a misread.

🧊 Rubik's Cube

Puzzles

Overview: A scrambled cube showing three faces (top, front-left, front-right). Enter the moves that solve it.

How to Solve: Paste the cube screenshot (Ctrl+V) — the 27 stickers are read and the shortest move sequence (up to 10 moves) is found and copied.

Speed Tip: If a sticker was misread, click it in the sticker net to cycle its colour — it re-solves instantly.
